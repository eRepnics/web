Stalevator

A low cost stair lift that takes support from the stairs unlike traditional stairlifts. The design currently uses a gear system, motors and two rods as a track for the chair to go up and down. It also has mobile app to manage stairlift movements, structure that is hinged on floor and not the wall.The overall idea is for safe transportation of locomotive disabled and elderly people between the floors in the building, making stairlifts a common tool for lacs of existing buildings like temples, apartments, offices etc.
